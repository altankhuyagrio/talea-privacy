Talea Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

Talea is a personal finance app for iPhone and iPad, made by [DEVELOPER NAME] (“we”). In short: your financial records stay on your device. Talea has no user accounts and no servers of ours, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. We cannot see your data.

What Talea stores, and where

  • Your records. Accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, people, tags, and the receipts and drafts you attach are saved in one file in Talea’s private storage on your device, next to a recovery copy of the previous save. Both are protected by iOS Data Protection: they are encrypted and can’t be opened while your device is locked.
  • Your preferences, such as currency, appearance, reminder times and the app lock, and the latest exchange-rate table, are kept in Talea’s settings on your device.
  • Items on their way in. When you share a receipt, PDF or text to Talea from another app, or the optional Apple Pay automation in Shortcuts reports a payment (merchant, amount and card name), the item waits in a folder on your device that only Talea and its Share extension can use. Talea deletes it once it has become a draft.
  • Device backups. Like other app data, Talea’s file is included in your iPhone or iPad backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on. Apple or your computer handles those backups, not us.

We don’t receive or keep a copy of any of this.

iCloud transfer (optional)

If you use Settings → iCloud transfer, Talea uploads a complete copy of your workspace (records, receipts and drafts) to the private database of your own iCloud account, so you can download it on your other iPhone or iPad. Nothing is uploaded or downloaded until you tap a button, and nothing syncs in the background.

Apple stores the data in your private iCloud database under your Apple Account, and Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy apply to it. We, the developer, can’t see or access it.

Exchange rates

The only server Talea contacts on its own is open.er-api.com, run by ExchangeRate-API, to download the day’s public exchange-rate table. Talea downloads the whole table for every currency, so the request doesn’t reveal which currencies you use. It carries no personal or financial data, no identifiers, no cookies and no cached data.

Like any internet request, it reaches the rate provider’s server from your device’s IP address. We never receive that address. ExchangeRate-API’s own terms and privacy policy apply to their service. You can turn the download off in Settings → Valuation rates → Update rates automatically and enter rates yourself.

Processing on your device

These features run on your device. Nothing they read is sent to us.

  • Receipt scanning. The document camera and text recognition (Apple’s VisionKit and Vision) read receipts on the device. Photos come from the system photo picker, which gives Talea only the photos you choose.
  • Dictation. Speech recognition runs only on the device. If your device can’t recognize your language offline, dictation is unavailable rather than sending audio anywhere. Audio is not saved.
  • Apple Intelligence. On supported devices, Talea uses Apple’s on-device language model to turn what you type, dictate or scan into transaction drafts.
  • App lock. iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID, Optic ID or passcode. Talea only learns whether unlocking succeeded and never sees biometric data.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Talea doesn’t use push notifications.
  • Imports. CSV files and bank statements you choose are read on the device.

No tracking, analytics or ads

Talea has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs. We don’t sell, rent or share data, because we don’t have any. Talea’s privacy manifest declares that it collects no data and does no tracking.

If you chose in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us crash reports and aggregated App Store statistics that don’t identify you. Apple’s privacy policy covers that.

Exports and backups

Export JSON backup and Export CSV save a file only where you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive. Talea doesn’t encrypt exported files, and once saved they are outside Talea, so keep them somewhere safe.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Erase local data deletes your records, receipts, drafts, the recovery copy and any items waiting to be imported on this device. Preferences such as appearance and currency are kept.
  • Deleting the app removes all of Talea’s data from the device.
  • Copies you made elsewhere are deleted where they are: exported files in Files; the iCloud copy in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → storage → Talea; older device backups in your backup settings.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have a question about your rights under data protection law, contact us.

Children

Talea is a general-audience finance app and is not directed at children. It doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll publish the new version at this address and update the date at the top. If a future version of Talea starts collecting data, we’ll update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
